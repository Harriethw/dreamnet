# How To Build Your Own DIY Internet

## Intro

### What is this?

A brief starter guide to learning about network infrastructure and servers, a spring board for diving deeper into the world of protocols, servers, ports and routers and such.

### Who is this for?

If you are curious about the idea of building your own server, setting up a wifi router, or building networks of any kind, this is a nice intro to create a proof-of-concept that you could build on. If you are at all nervous about any of the words used already, please be reassured that that’s totally natural! There’s a lot of jargon and opaque language around these tools, but there’s also lots of amazing resources out there to learn. 

That said, I won’t be explaining every step in detail - I’m assuming some knowledge of things like using command line interfaces, how to edit files in the terminal, and a basic mental model of a raspberry pi/installing software via ssh. If you do get stuck don’t panic! Making mistakes and breaking things is a great way to learn, and the answer is probably a short Google or youtube video away. I’m also happy to answer any questions - you can contact me on harriethw at gmail dot com or on mastodon at https://mas.to/@harriethw 

## Why?

Aside from it just being fun to learn and build things, I do think that it is important to at least imagine a world where our infrastructure is not built by Big Tech. With the climate emergency, rise of fascism, and even just the expense of technology, reclaiming the networks we use to communicate on could be a useful tool for communities.
